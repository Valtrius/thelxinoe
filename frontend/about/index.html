Thelxinoe Privacy Policy

Thelxinoe

A self-hosted media server with web and desktop clients for your media library, YouTube subscriptions and live streams.

Source code and installation Connected accounts

Library and playback

Browse media on your server, play it from the web or desktop app, and save viewing progress. The server stores accounts, settings and viewing records for the people who use that installation.

Videos and live streams

Connect YouTube to browse subscription videos, connect Twitch to see followed channels that are live, or track Kick channels by name. Save videos to Thelxinoe watchlists and resume playback later.

Connected accounts

Thelxinoe uses YouTube API Services with read-only permission to retrieve your YouTube channel identity, subscriptions, and channel and video metadata. It does not use this permission to upload videos, post comments or change subscriptions.

You authorize access on Google's own sign-in screen. Your Thelxinoe server stores the resulting connection credentials in encrypted form so it can refresh your feed. Each person connects their own account; the server administrator configures the Google application.

The Privacy Policy explains what is stored, who operates the server, and how to disconnect accounts or request data removal.

Terms of use

Use connected services in accordance with their terms and the permissions for the content you access. By using Thelxinoe's YouTube features, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Thelxinoe is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, YouTube, Twitch or Kick.

Support and privacy questions

For account access or removal of data on this installation, contact the administrator who provides your Thelxinoe account. For software issues, use the project issue tracker. Do not include credentials or private account information in public issues.
