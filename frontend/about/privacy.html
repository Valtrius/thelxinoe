Privacy Policy

Connected accounts, server storage and your viewing information.

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Who operates your installation

Thelxinoe is self-hosted software for media libraries, YouTube subscriptions and live streams. This policy describes the software's data handling, including its web and desktop clients and these informational pages. The person or organization running your server controls that installation, its storage, backups and access.

Your account data is held by that server, not automatically sent to the Thelxinoe project maintainer. If someone else hosts your server, ask them about their hosting, access and retention practices. Server administrators with access to its files and encryption keys can access stored information.

Connected accounts and permissions

Google and YouTube

Thelxinoe uses YouTube API Services. When you connect an account, it requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly. It retrieves your YouTube channel identifier, display name and avatar; subscriptions; and channel and video metadata such as titles, thumbnails, publication dates, durations and live status.

This data provides your subscription feed, account display and video details. The permission does not let Thelxinoe upload videos, post comments or change your subscriptions. Google handles sign-in and consent; Thelxinoe does not ask for your Google password.

Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted on your Thelxinoe server and used to make authorized requests and renew the connection. The server also stores its configured Google application credentials encrypted. Account metadata, subscriptions and viewing records are stored separately in its database.

Twitch and Kick

Twitch connections use user:read:follows to retrieve account identity and followed channels and show live streams. Kick channels are tracked from names or URLs you enter; optional administrator-configured application credentials retrieve public channel metadata without signing into a viewer's Kick account.

Information stored and used

The server stores Thelxinoe accounts and sessions, connected-account details, cached media and channel metadata, subscriptions, tracked channels, watchlists, viewing progress, viewing statistics, settings, and requested downloads. Operational and audit logs can contain account or media identifiers, file paths and error details.

Web and desktop clients retain session information and preferences needed to use the server. The browser uses a session cookie for sign-in and a short-lived cookie during YouTube authorization. Playback tools process media URLs and stream data to play or download the media you request. YouTube subscription authorization is separate from any playback authentication configured in supporting tools.

Network access and sharing

Thelxinoe communicates with your configured server, Google/YouTube, Twitch, Kick and their media and image delivery services for the features you use. Requests disclose the necessary identifiers, authorization information and ordinary connection details, such as IP addresses and request times, to their recipients. Configured media integrations, metadata providers and software update services also receive requests when used.

Thelxinoe does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or train generalized AI models with it. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used for the account and media features described here.

External services operate under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, Twitch Privacy Notice and Kick Privacy Policy.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Use Disconnect in Thelxinoe's YouTube account controls to remove the saved authorization credentials and stop authorized synchronization. Disconnecting does not erase all previously stored account metadata, subscriptions, watchlists, viewing records or downloads.

You can separately revoke Thelxinoe's grant through Google Account connections, or revoke Twitch access through Twitch connections. Revoking access at a provider does not itself delete existing data on your Thelxinoe server.

Remove saved items and downloads using their app controls. For complete account-data removal, contact your server administrator. Stored records and files may remain until removed by you, the administrator or application cleanup. Backups and exported files follow the operator's retention practices and may require separate deletion. If you run the server yourself, you control its persistent storage and backups.

These public pages

The home page and privacy policy are static informational pages. They do not require sign-in or contain analytics, advertising, account forms or embedded media. Their content can be read without JavaScript, and they do not query your account or viewing history.

The hosting server and reverse proxy receive ordinary web-request information and may retain operational or security logs. These pages do not add tracking cookies. The operator controls hosting and proxy behavior.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, access to your data or deletion requests, contact the administrator who provides your Thelxinoe account, using the support contact shown on the Google consent screen for this installation where applicable. The software maintainer cannot remotely inspect or erase an independently hosted server.

Software issues can be reported through the Thelxinoe issue tracker. Public issues are visible to others; do not submit tokens, credentials or private account information there. Information you voluntarily send for support is handled by its recipient.

This policy's effective date changes when it is revised. Thelxinoe is independent of Google, YouTube, Twitch and Kick.
